## Why We Built It

> "Tell me and I forget. Teach me and I remember. Involve me and I learn."

That's the idea behind Try Sailing. Getting stuck in the no-go zone can be puzzling when you're new to sailing. Here, you can turn the boat, adjust the sails, and discover what gets you moving again, all on dry land. It's a chance to learn by doing before your first day on the water.

If you already sail, share a link with a friend or let someone scan the built-in QR code at your club. Explore the basics together, on a phone, tablet, or computer.

## Discover How Sailing Works

Why can't a boat sail straight into the wind? When should you pull a sail in, and when should you let it out?

This simple simulator gives you a place to experiment with those questions. Turn towards the wind and discover the **no-go zone**. Bear away, adjust your **sail trim**, and watch how the boat's speed changes. Try **tacking**, turning the bow through the wind, and **gybing**, turning the stern through the wind.

The instruments show your point of sail and highlight the no-go zone. On-screen guidance helps you recognise when a sail is luffing or pulled in too tightly, and the simulator acknowledges completed tacks and gybes. Simple telltales and wind indicators give you more clues about what the wind and sails are doing.

You can watch from outside the boat or switch to a first-person view to see things from the sailor's perspective. Take your time, make a change, and watch how the boat responds.

## Two Boats, Different Things to Practise

### [Hansa 303](https://www.marineverse.com/try-sailing/hansa-303): Main and Jib

We shared the story behind [bringing the Hansa 303 to MarineVerse Sailing Club]({% post_url 2025-12-19-hansa-303-joins-marineverse-sailing-club %}) last year. Now you can meet it in your browser, too.

The Hansa 303 has a **mainsail and a jib**, giving you two sails to manage independently. Experiment with their trim and notice how it affects both speed and steering: the balance between the sails can influence where the boat wants to turn.

**[Sail the Hansa 303 →](https://www.marineverse.com/try-sailing/hansa-303)**

### [Opti](https://www.marineverse.com/try-sailing/opti): Get a Feel for the Tiller

The Opti has a **single mainsail and tiller steering**. Push the tiller left to turn right; push it right to turn left. That opposite-direction movement can feel confusing at first, and the simulator gives beginners a place to practise until it starts to feel more natural.

With just one sail to manage, there's more room to focus on steering.

**[Sail the Opti →](https://www.marineverse.com/try-sailing/opti)**

You can use **a keyboard, touch or mouse controls, or a compatible dual-stick gamepad**. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys to steer and trim the mainsail, with R/F for the Hansa's jib. On a phone or tablet, the two on-screen sticks put the controls under your thumbs.

## Follow the Stars

For a little extra purpose, try collecting stars.

Each new star gives you another destination to sail towards, encouraging you to change course, sail at different angles to the wind, and adjust your trim along the way. It's a simple way to put the basics into practice while playing.

Or just sail around and experiment. There's no rush.

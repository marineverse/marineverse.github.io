Welcome to the twenty ninth post in the series **"VR Sailors - MarineVerse Community"** – introducing sailors in [MarineVerse Sailing Club](https://www.marineverse.com/sailing-club-app).

Today, we are chatting with **Mark**, known online as **Redshank**.

<!--more-->

In virtual reality, you can meet Mark as [Redshank](https://www.marineverse.com/members/HEY5ZzqxiYaJ0P4WPTOZS0q6GLtXU7XLyjcTIIgM4zDw5rE). He sails his cruiser [Redshank](https://www.marineverse.com/globe/boats-profiles/87274d27-786d-43c5-9002-8811c2af516f) in MarineVerse Globe, which is part of the MarineVerse Sailing Club app.

We asked Mark a few questions.


**What is your sailing experience?**

*I do not have any sailing experience.*

**What is your favourite sailing memory?**

*As a child, I was once invited to join our neighbours on a day trip in a small open sailing boat, which I enjoyed very much.*

**Tell us something about your experience with MarineVerse Sailing Club. Any good memories or highlights?**

*Some years ago, I discovered MarineVerse Cup—now called MarineVerse Sailing Club—after buying all sorts of games for my newly acquired Quest 2.*

*I am not a gamer, and I soon got bored with most of the games on my Quest. MarineVerse Sailing Club was the exception.*

*It felt very special to be able to learn some basic sailing skills and take part in the dinghy races.*

*When I heard about [MarineVerse Globe](https://blog.marineverse.com/marineverse/globe/2025/08/14/introducing-marineverse-globe-beta.html), I immediately bought a Quest 3, and I have been enjoying it ever since. In fact, it is the only game I use on my Quest 3.*

*I especially like the live weather conditions, changing currents, and light signals from buoys and lighthouses.*

*For instance, it is a fantastic challenge to tack through the English Channel with strong, unfavourable winds and currents. There is an enormous variety of waves and light beacons in the Channel.*

*I find the overall rendering of clouds and the ocean very satisfying, especially considering the limitations of the Quest 3.*

<img src="/assets/community/redshank_tracks.jpg" alt="Redshank's sailing tracks in MarineVerse Globe" style="max-width: 700px; width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto; display: block;"/>

**Who should try MarineVerse Sailing Club, and why?**

*I think MarineVerse Sailing Club will appeal to anyone with even the slightest interest in sailing. It is a great way to learn basic sailing skills.*

**Any tips for those new to sailing who want to race in MarineVerse?**

*I am not a great racer myself, but I have noticed that my sailing improves noticeably when I sail more often.*

**Anything else you'd like to share?**

*MarineVerse Globe is a great tool for learning to navigate with a paper chart, plotter, and dividers.*

*A halo-style head strap is much more comfortable. Removing the facial interface also allows you to peek underneath the VR headset at a chart while keeping an eye on the boat.*

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Melbourne, Australia – March 17, 2026** – A champion has been crowned at the **world's first International Hansa 303 Virtual Sailing Challenge**, held using [**MarineVerse Sailing Club**](https://www.meta.com/en-gb/experiences/marineverse-sailing-club/2465388190184288), the virtual reality sailing platform designed to make sailing more accessible, engaging and social.

<!--more-->

The inaugural event marked a significant step forward for accessible sailing, introducing virtual competition to [the Hansa Class](https://hansaclass.org/) community and creating an exciting new way for sailors, volunteers and spectators to engage with the sport.

## World's First Virtual Reality Hansa Class Competition

Held alongside the **[2026 SKUD 18 International Match Race Challenge](https://sailabilityauckland.org.nz/public/schedule/events/76486-2026-skud-18-international-match-race-challenge) in Auckland — itself another world first** — the International Hansa 303 Virtual Sailing Challenge quickly became a focal point of activity onshore. While sailors rotated on and off the water during racing, volunteers, caregivers and spectators gathered around the Virtual Reality setup to watch and take part in the virtual regatta.

<iframe width="100%" height="415" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/xkF4gbWTFAY?rel=0&amp;showinfo=0" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen></iframe>

The competition consisted of a **[race time trial](https://www.marineverse.com/events/hansa-nz-1/races/F80C0VpHsPSJiX7-p2OxvQPQXhRz6QrGitLouiIUmlwVuMk)**, with competitors attempting to record the fastest lap through [the virtual course](https://www.marineverse.com/events/hansa-nz-1/races/F80C0VpHsPSJiX7-p2OxvQPQXhRz6QrGitLouiIUmlwVuMk/map2d). After a short introduction to the controls and tutorial sailing session, participants moved into race mode where their results were recorded on the event leaderboard.

Many players were experiencing Virtual Reality for the first time, but quickly became comfortable with the technology. The large screen displaying live gameplay, along with the iPad leaderboard, created a lively atmosphere as spectators watched competitors attempt to climb the rankings.


**Jack Wallace of Australia** emerged as the **inaugural International Hansa 303 Virtual Sailing Challenge Champion**, recording the fastest race time of the event.

Our warmest congratulations to Jack for his outstanding performance. Sponsors generously provided **great prizes for the top competitors**, with a few Australian sailors reportedly needing a little extra baggage space for their trip home.

> "We're extremely happy with how engaging this Virtual Reality competition was for caregivers, visitors and sponsors who don't usually get to experience racing first hand. Combined with live event tracking, it really brought the sailing to life for those watching from shore and may have set a new benchmark for inclusion at disabled sailing events."
> — **Paul Wager**, Event Organiser

> "MarineVerse Sailing Club is about making sailing more accessible and more engaging, whether someone is on the water, learning on shore, or experiencing the sport for the first time. It was fantastic to see the Hansa class community embrace virtual sailing as part of the event experience."
> — **Greg Dziemidowicz**, CEO of MarineVerse

## Looking Ahead

The success of the event demonstrated how **Virtual Reality sailing can complement real-world regattas**, providing an engaging activity onshore while racing takes place on the water.

Following the success of this world-first competition, organisers believe the **International Hansa 303 Virtual Sailing Challenge** could become a regular feature at sailing events around the world, offering a new way for people to experience the sport and connect with the global sailing community.
